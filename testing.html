Emotion: FOMO
Notes: "Everyone was buying, jumped in late"
MISTAKEClassic late FOMO entry

Your own notes say "everyone was buying" — that's the signal to not enter, not to enter. When retail piles in, institutions are already distributing. You entered at ₹47,200 when the move started at ₹46,600. Zero edge.

Risk
High
PATTERNSL too tight for BankNifty ATR

₹200 SL on BankNifty — which moves 400-600 points on a normal day. Price noise alone hit your SL before the trade could work. ATR-based stops would have changed the outcome completely.

ACTIONWait for the retest, always

Same direction, better price, tighter SL, higher R:R. Wait for price to retest the breakout level instead of chasing. This one habit change can improve your win rate by 15-20%.
