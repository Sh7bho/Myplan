SAMPLE ANALYSIS

This is what you get.

A real breakdown. Not a generic tip. Specific to your trade.

TRADE INPUT
Symbol: BANKNIFTY
Direction: LONG · Market: Indian F&O
Entry: ₹47,200 · Exit: ₹46,850
SL: ₹47,000 · Target: ₹47,800
Emotion: FOMO
Notes: "Everyone was buying, I jumped in late"
MISTAKE You chased the move

Your own note says "everyone was buying" — that's a classic FOMO entry. When retail traders pile in late, institutions are already distributing. Entering at ₹47,200 when the move started at ₹46,600 meant you had zero edge.

Risk
High
PATTERN Your SL was too tight for the setup

You set your SL at ₹47,000 — only 200 points below entry on BankNifty, which has an average daily range of 400-600 points. Normal price noise hit your SL before the trade could play out. ATR-based stops would have saved this trade.

ACTION Wait for the retest next time

Instead of entering on breakout momentum, wait for a retest of the breakout level. Same direction, better price, tighter SL, higher R:R. This one habit change alone could improve your win rate by 15-20%.
